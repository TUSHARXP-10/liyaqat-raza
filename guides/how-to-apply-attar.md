# How to apply attar so it lasts all day

Attar is perfume oil, and it is worn differently from a spray. A single drop, placed well, can carry you through the day. Here is how we suggest wearing it.

## 1. Start with clean, dry skin

Apply attar after a bath, once your skin is dry. Fragrance holds better on skin that is clean and lightly moisturised: an unscented moisturiser helps if your skin is dry, because dry skin lets fragrance fade faster.

## 2. Use less than you think

One drop, or one stroke of the applicator, is usually enough for each spot. Attar is concentrated. Too much can feel heavy, especially in a closed room or in the heat.

## 3. Choose warm pulse points

Pulse points are where blood runs close to the skin. Their warmth gently lifts the fragrance through the day.

- **Inside the wrists**
- **Behind the ears** and the sides of the neck
- The **base of the throat**
- **Inside the elbows**

For men who wear attar with a beard, a touch on the jawline, not on the beard itself, works well.

## 4. Dab, don’t rub

Touch the attar on and let it settle. Rubbing your wrists together warms and breaks up the top notes, so the opening fades faster.

## 5. On clothes: test first

Traditionally, attar is also applied to clothes, often on a small cotton ball tucked into a pocket or on the inside of a collar or cuff. Fragrance oils can mark light or delicate fabrics, so try a hidden spot first, and keep attar away from silk and pale colours.

## 6. Layering for longer wear

To make a fragrance last into the evening, wear the **attar first** and spray the **perfume of the same fragrance** lightly on top. The oil anchors the spray. Every Raza fragrance is available both ways, so layering the same scent is easy.

## 7. Heat, humidity and the Indian summer

Heat makes fragrance bloom faster, so in summer:

- Use a little less than you would in winter.
- Favour fresher scents by day and keep deep ouds and ambers for evenings.
- Reapply one small drop in the afternoon rather than a lot in the morning.

In the cooler months, rich oud, amber, musk and sandalwood attars come into their own. See our [oud collection](/oud).

## 8. Store it well

Keep attar bottles **closed, upright and away from sunlight and heat**: not on a sunny windowsill or in a hot car. A cupboard or drawer is ideal. Stored this way, a good attar keeps its character for a long time.
