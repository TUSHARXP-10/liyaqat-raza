    III Our Story

    Established in 1986

    What began in 1986 as a small shop, fueled by a deep love for fragrances, has grown into Raza Perfume — a trusted name across generations. More than just scents, we create emotions, memories, and moments that last forever.

    1986 The Founder

    Yasinali Sayyad

    In 1986, Yasinali Sayyad opened a small perfume counter in Bombay with a handful of attars and a deep love for the craft. Blending oud, musk, amber, rose and sandal by hand, he treated every customer like family — and built a name his family still carries today.

    IV The House Today

    Liyaqat & Yasmeen

    Sayyad · Founders & Owners, Raza Perfume

    Liyaqat on LinkedIn

    What Yasinali began at a single counter in 1986, Liyaqat and Yasmeen Sayyad carry forward — together, as partners in life and in craft. Side by side, they founded Raza Perfume as it stands today, shaping that legacy into a house of rare oud, musk and amber, made with the same devotion, and the same promise that every bottle carries a story worth wearing.
